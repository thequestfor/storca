## Primary source and audit scope

Sander et al., *Chemical Kinetics and Photochemical Data for Use in Atmospheric
Studies*, Evaluation No. 17, JPL Publication 10-6 (2011). The original 684-page
PDF was obtained through the [official NASA/JPL evaluations index](https://science.jpl.nasa.gov/projects/data-evaluation/)
and [official previous-evaluation download](https://science.jpl.nasa.gov/documents/1489/JPL_10-6_Final_15June2011_20110620.pdf).
`source-audit.json` records its exact artifact, SHA256, access provenance and
selected-page visual audit. Relevant complete pages were rendered with Poppler
and visually checked, corroborating text extraction; malformed-stream warnings
elsewhere do not establish that every publication page was audited.

The checked scope includes publication identity; Section 1.2 temperature-envelope
semantics; Section 2 units, air bath, falloff and estimation assumptions;
Table 2-1 B1/B2/D2/D3 limiting-rate rows and table footnotes; and complete notes
on collider sensitivity, the competing OH channel, exponent signs and air
corrections. The audit supplies no new independent experiment.

## Observed reference defect and amendment

The table's limiting coefficients are anchored at 300 K, but its multiplicative
reference factors are f(298). The manifest copied f(298) into eight 300 K points.
The published correction is:

```text
f(T) = f(298) * exp(g * abs(1/T - 1/298))
```

| Recommendation | f(298) | g / K | Corrected f(300) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| H + O2 association, B1 | 1.3 | 50 | 1.30145495228246 |
| OH + OH association, B2 | 1.5 | 100 | 1.5033594610833196 |
| CH3 + O2 association, D2 | 1.1 | 50 | 1.1012311134697739 |
| C2H5 + O2 association, D3 | 1.2 | 50 | 1.2013430328761168 |

Only these eight reference factors and explicit uncertainty/provenance semantics
were amended. Central rates, physical conditions, partitions and acceptance
thresholds are unchanged. `pre-amendment-manifest.json` preserves the actual
original bytes; its SHA256 is checked by
`test_jpl_reference_factors_follow_the_audited_temperature_equation`. Removing
the amendment and restoring the old factors also reproduces the prior semantic
document, preventing unrelated retuning from being hidden in this correction.

`failing-before-002.txt` records eight incorrect factor assertions plus four

## What the references do not establish

- Four reaction-level evaluated recommendations supply eight limiting
  coefficients; they are not eight independent experimental calibration cases.
- Evaluator-assigned envelopes do not specify an exact confidence probability,
  independent error distributions for k0/kinf, or independent errors for each
  pressure/temperature point. The source's approximate statistical descriptions
  do not justify assigning an exact Gaussian probability.
- Air-bath recommendations are not N2-only or arbitrary humid-gas references.
  H+O2 collider sensitivity, OH+OH's competing H2O+O channel, and the ethyl+O2
  evaluated air correction remain explicit applicability concerns.
- A curve generated from the evaluated broadening formula is a model comparator,
  not independently measured finite-pressure data or a prospective holdout.
- These data do not calibrate STORCA electronic structure, conformer treatment,
  capture potentials, collisional energy transfer or mechanism coverage. No
  specialist backend/lane support, P1 result or first-ten closure is promoted.
