## What follows scientifically

The requested 423.15 K, nominal initial 50 mm Hg development case is locally
consistent with that provisional description. This is a reason to investigate
the approximation, not a bound on its error.

A plateau over a finite observed pressure interval cannot, by itself, establish
how close the measurements are to the asymptotic limiting rate. Quantifying that
closeness requires source-audited numerical pressure-rate evidence and
uncertainties plus a defensible limiting-rate/falloff inference. Small observed
variation cannot be substituted for unknown total or asymptotic error. The
registered two-row spread is neither of those bounds.

Even a valid bound for the measured overall singlet ring opening would not
validate the pressure treatment of every retained channel, especially the two
electronically unresolved triplet reactions. The current constant-rate native
integration proves numerical consistency of that named model, not collisionally
thermalized populations or physically pressure-independent elementary rates.
