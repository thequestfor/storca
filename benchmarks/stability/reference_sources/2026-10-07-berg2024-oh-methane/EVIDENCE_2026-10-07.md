# Primary experimental OH + methane rate slice

This separate descriptive development slice retains the original primary
paper and supplement from Berg et al. (2024), DOI 10.5194/acp-24-13715-2024.
It does not merge into or enlarge the shared eleven-case manifest plus optional
P1, delete pending failures, create a holdout or authorize chemistry execution.

All fourteen individual methane coefficients in supplement Table S1 are
transcribed with temperature, canister group and printed plus/minus values.
The first original table row is selected by table order, before any prediction:
1.40e-14 cm^3 molecule^-1 s^-1 at 340.3 K, nominal 1000 hPa (1 bar) reaction
pressure, in humidified synthetic air. This is total bimolecular OH loss, not a
per-H elementary path or a pseudo-first-order coefficient. Preparation-canister
and detector pressures are not substituted for reaction pressure.

The fourteen points belong to one study with shared systematics, not fourteen
independent calibration systems. Supplement point sigma definitions, full
covariance, total uncertainty and exact air fractions are unresolved. The main
methods' 12% two-sigma accuracy and the different printed room-temperature
summary uncertainty are both retained; neither is chosen to improve scoring.
No pressure independence, HPL applicability, product mechanism or lifetime is
inferred. The request exports molecular identity and reported conditions only,
with no reference products, rates, sources or fabricated compute budget.
